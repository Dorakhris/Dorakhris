# Cyber Threat Intelligence & Investigations

I'm Dorathy Christopher, a cybersecurity professional focused on cyber threat intelligence, OSINT, and investigations.

I enjoy researching cyber threats, following digital evidence, connecting information from different sources, and working out what a threat means to the organisation or person involved.

My work covers threat research, OSINT investigations, threat actor profiling, MITRE ATT&CK, OpenCTI, digital risk, and intelligence reporting. I also have experience in digital forensics, security monitoring, and GRC, which I use when an investigation needs a wider technical or risk view.
